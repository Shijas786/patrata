      Open by design

      For people

      No account, no tracking, no server. Close the tab and nothing about you exists anywhere but this browser.

      For state government schemes, visit Rajya Desk, or ask the assistant: it asks Rajya Desk for you, after you agree.

      For agents and other sites

      This is an open agentic website. Any agent site can call its network, and its eligibility check runs in the caller's own browser.

      • agent-manifest.json — who we are
      • patrata.network.json — the agent network
      • schemes.json — every rule, as open data
      • links.json — linked networks

      For builders

      Run a desk for your state, district or NGO: write your schemes in the same rule format, and every other desk's agents can ask yours.
